Automations

Six places AI saves time and money — and what was actually measured.

Pick a workflow to see what we’d automate, and beside it what the research found when someone tested it.

Support triage & reply drafting

Incoming tickets and emails are sorted, routed to the right person and paired with a suggested reply drawn from your own knowledge base and past resolutions. Your agent reviews it and presses send.

What gets automated

  • Categorising and prioritising new tickets
  • Drafting replies from your documentation and previous answers
  • Summarising long threads when a case is handed over
  • Flagging anything that needs a human straight away

Where the saving comes from

More issues closed per hour by the same team, and new starters reaching the standard of experienced colleagues sooner, because the system surfaces what your best people already know.

Peer-reviewed study · 5,172 support agents

15%

more issues resolved per hour

“Access to AI assistance increases worker productivity, as measured by issues resolved per hour, by 15% on average, with substantial heterogeneity across workers.”

Brynjolfsson, Li & Raymond, Generative AI at Work, Quarterly Journal of Economics, 2025 1

Worth knowing: the gains were uneven. The earlier working paper reported a 34% improvement for novice and lower-skilled agents, while the most experienced agents gained little.

Document, report & email drafting

First drafts of proposals, reports, policies, job adverts and customer emails are generated from your templates, notes and data. Your people edit and approve instead of starting from a blank page.

What gets automated

  • First drafts written in your house style and structure
  • Turning rough notes or bullet points into finished prose
  • Rewriting one document for different audiences
  • Proofreading, tightening and consistency checks

Where the saving comes from

Time moves from producing a first draft to improving it. In the study opposite, people finished faster and independent graders rated the work higher.

Peer-reviewed experiment · 453 professionals

40%

less time on writing tasks

“Access to the assistive chatbot ChatGPT decreased the time it took workers to complete the tasks by 40 percent, and output quality rose by 18 percent.”

MIT News, summarising Noy & Zhang, Science, 2023 2

Worth knowing: the tasks were short, self-contained pieces of 20–30 minutes. Work that needs careful fact-checking will see smaller gains.

Meetings, inbox & everyday admin

The small tasks that fill a working day: writing up meetings, catching up on long email threads, updating records, finding the right document. Individually minor, collectively hours a week.

What gets automated

  • Meeting notes, decisions and action lists
  • Summaries of long emails and document chains
  • Routine record updates and status reports
  • Searching across files for the answer to a question

Where the saving comes from

A little time back for every person, every day. In the government trial opposite, 26 minutes a day added up to roughly 13 working days per person over a year.

UK government trial · 20,000 civil servants

26 min

saved per person, per day

“Trial participants saved an average of 26 minutes a day when using M365 Copilot.”

Government Digital Service, Microsoft 365 Copilot Experiment: Cross-Government Findings Report, 2025 3

Worth knowing: savings were self-reported, and 17% of users noticed no clear time saving at all. More than a third saved over half an hour a day.

Invoice & accounts-payable processing

Invoices arriving as PDFs, scans or emails are read automatically, matched against purchase orders and routed for approval. People deal with the exceptions rather than keying in every line.

What gets automated

  • Capturing and extracting invoice data
  • Matching invoices to purchase orders and receipts
  • Routing approvals to the right person
  • Flagging duplicates, mismatches and missing details

Where the saving comes from

Lower cost per invoice and far shorter cycle times, which also means fewer late-payment problems and a clearer view of cash going out.

Industry benchmark · accounts payable

$2.78

per invoice at the most automated teams, versus $12.88 elsewhere

The best-performing accounts-payable teams process an invoice in 3.1 days for $2.78. Everyone else averages 17.4 days and $12.88.

Ardent Partners, AP Metrics that Matter in 2025, as reported by ERP Today 4

Worth knowing: this is a US benchmark comparing teams by how automated they are overall, not a controlled trial of AI. It shows the size of the gap, not a guaranteed result.

Feedback, survey & free-text analysis

Thousands of open-text responses — surveys, reviews, complaints, consultation replies — grouped into themes, counted and summarised with representative quotes. An analyst checks the themes rather than reading every line.

What gets automated

  • Reading and tagging every response
  • Grouping responses into themes and counting them
  • Pulling out representative quotes
  • Producing a first-draft summary for review

Where the saving comes from

Days of manual reading become hours of checking, and you can afford to analyse feedback you’d otherwise only sample.

UK government estimate · “Consult” tool

75,000

days of manual analysis a year

“Across the 500 consultations the government runs annually, the tool could help save officials from around 75,000 days of analysis every year, which costs the government £20 million in staffing costs.”

Department for Science, Innovation and Technology, press release, 14 May 2025 5

Worth knowing: this is the government’s own estimate for a tool that was still being trialled, not an audited saving.

Software & internal tools

AI coding assistants for the scripts, integrations, dashboards and small internal tools that connect your systems — the glue work that never quite reaches the top of the list.

What gets automated

  • Boilerplate, scaffolding and routine code
  • Scripts that move data between systems
  • Tests and documentation
  • Explaining unfamiliar or inherited code

Where the saving comes from

Well-defined, self-contained tasks get built faster. The evidence opposite is deliberately two-sided: on large, mature codebases, experienced developers were slowed down.

Controlled experiment · GitHub Copilot

55.8%

faster on a defined coding task

“The treatment group, with access to the AI pair programmer, completed the task 55.8% faster than the control group.”

Peng, Kalliamvakou, Cihon & Demirer, 2023 6

The counterpoint: a 2025 randomised trial of 16 experienced open-source developers working in their own mature projects found AI tools made them 19% slower 7. Context decides the result.

What we take from this

AI delivers real, measurable savings on the right tasks, and can quietly cost you on the wrong ones. The difference isn’t the tool. It’s choosing the task carefully, keeping a person in the loop, and measuring before and after. That is the whole of our approach.

Approach

Small, measured steps. No big-bang transformation.

Four stages, each with a clear output, so you always know what you’re getting and whether it’s working.

  1. 01

    Map

    We look at where your team’s time actually goes and shortlist the repetitive, high-volume tasks where the evidence says AI helps.

    You get a ranked list of automation candidates

  2. 02

    Baseline

    Before changing anything, we record how long the task takes and what it costs today. Without a baseline, any claimed saving is a guess.

    You get today’s time and cost, written down

  3. 03

    Pilot

    We build one automation for one workflow, working with the tools you already use, with a person reviewing the output.

    You get a working automation in real use

  4. 04

    Measure & scale

    We compare against the baseline. If it pays, we extend it to the next workflow. If it doesn’t, we tell you plainly and stop.

    You get a before-and-after result you can trust

People stay in the loop

The research is clear that AI makes mistakes on tasks outside its strengths. We design every automation so a person checks what matters.

Built around what you have

The aim is to add a layer on top of your existing systems and processes, not to make you replace software that already works.

Data handled with care

We think about what data an automation touches and where it goes before building anything, with UK data-protection law in mind from the start.

Questions worth asking us.

You’re new. Why should we talk to you?

Fair question. We can’t point to a track record yet, so we’ve built our approach to not need your trust up front: start with one small pilot, measure it against a baseline, and judge us on the result. Everything we claim about AI on this site is sourced, so you can check it rather than take our word for it.

Do you have case studies or client references?

Not yet. LABMISE has only just started, and we won’t publish invented or anonymised “success stories”. When we have real client results, they’ll appear here with the client’s permission.

Will the savings in these studies apply to our business?

Not automatically. The figures come from specific teams doing specific tasks, and results vary widely, even within the same study. That is why we record a baseline first and measure your own before-and-after rather than quoting someone else’s number.

Is this about replacing staff?

The studies we cite measured people working with AI: the same team getting through more, or getting time back. In the British Chambers of Commerce’s 2026 research, 95% of SMEs using AI said it had not changed the size of their workforce over the past year 11. How you use the time saved is your decision.

Does AI always help?

No, and anyone who says so hasn’t read the research. One trial found experienced developers were 19% slower with AI tools 7; another found consultants were more likely to get a task wrong when it fell outside what AI does well 8. Picking the right task matters more than picking the right tool.

Do we need to replace our existing software?

That isn’t the goal. We aim to work with the systems you already have and add automation on top. If something can’t be done sensibly with your current setup, we’ll say so before any work starts.
